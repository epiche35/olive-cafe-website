About Olive Cafe

A Welcoming Dining Destination in Dharan

Olive Cafe is situated along Putali Line in Dharan-10, offering a friendly, relaxed gathering place for friends, families, and food enthusiasts across Dharan.

Specializing in authentic firewood pizza baked with a thin, blistered crust and fresh toppings, our kitchen pairs rustic Italian favorites with beloved local tastes. From sizzling chicken lollipops and chicken satay skewers to our signature crispy Nepali C-momo and whole grilled spiced fish platter, we take pride in quality ingredients and hearty flavors.

Whether you are catching up over handcrafted barista latte art coffee on our open-air evening patio or dropping by for delicious pasta and burgers, we look forward to making every visit special.
